The portal

Your program, visible.

Every customer gets a login. Pricing, loads, statements, payments and impact — updated as loads ship, not assembled when you ask.

Pricing

Your current price on every grade, in one place, with the basis it rides on. No calling to ask what you're getting.

Loads

Every load with date, ticket number, grade, net weight and value — from scheduled through shipped to paid — filtered by site, month or grade.

Statements

A monthly statement per site, downloadable as a PDF, with a running balance that ties to your payments.

Payments

What's been paid and what's coming, with dates and the loads each payment covers. No chasing accounts payable.

Environmental Impact

Tons recycled, trees, water, energy and CO₂e by site and month — exportable for your own sustainability reporting.

Documents

The Marcells scale ticket and bill of lading for every load, available to open or download the moment it's weighed.

Updated as loads ship

Every load posts to your login when it crosses our scale. Status moves from scheduled to shipped to paid without a phone call.

Built for multi-site programs

One login can cover every plant in your network, with the same grades, the same reporting and the same people behind it — or a separate login per site if that's how you run.
