Marcells Pallet Inc

Pallets

Marcells Pallet Inc, our sister company, builds, repairs, recycles and sells wooden pallets for the same customers MPM serves for paper, metal and plastics. One pickup, one invoice, everything together.

What we do

  • Buy back and recycle your used and broken pallets
  • Repair and return pallets to service
  • Build new pallets to your spec
  • Sell new and recycled wooden pallets by the truckload

Why it works with a paper program

Pallets leave your building on the same trucks as your paper and metal. Putting them on the same program means fewer vendors, fewer trucks and one statement to reconcile.
