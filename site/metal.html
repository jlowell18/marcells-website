Materials

Metal

MPM isn't only paper. We collect and recycle metal waste from printers and manufacturers — aluminum printing plates above all, plus mixed metals and bulky items — with the equipment to strip, load and haul.

Why aluminum plates are worth recycling

Aluminum keeps its physical properties through recycling, so recycled and virgin aluminum are effectively the same material. Recycling plates reduces reliance on bauxite ore and uses a fraction of the energy of primary production — and it pays.

How it works

We drop containers of the right size at your facility, you fill them with plates and other metal (and paper), and we collect on a schedule that fits your production. Every load is weighed on our computerized scale and posted to your portal.

Construction and industrial metal

Any project produces metal waste that shouldn't go to a landfill and has to be handled correctly. We take care of collection and recycling so the material ends up where it should, with the paperwork to show it.
