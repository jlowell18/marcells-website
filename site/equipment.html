Equipment

The right equipment, sized to your tonnage.

Loose material on trucks is what costs you money; the equipment is how that stops. We size it to what your facility actually produces and support it for the life of the program.

Balers

Vertical and horizontal, matched to your monthly tonnage.

Conveyors

Feed from the floor or the line straight into the baler.

Roll Cutters

For converters and printers running rolls and trim.

Cart Tippers

Empty tubs and carts into the baler without lifting.

Compactors

For sites where volume calls for it.

Containers

Gaylord boxes, plastic tubs, collapsible containers, bin receptacles and spotted trailers dropped at your facility.

Financing

For sites with the right volume and deal structure, we finance balers, compactors and conveying equipment within the program. Terms are discussed site by site and never published.

How sizing works

A site analysis based on the tonnage your facility produces. Equipment matched to that tonnage — not the biggest machine we can place — with training and ongoing support included.
