Equipment

The right equipment, financed within your program.

Balers, conveyors, cart tippers and roll cutters — sized to your tonnage, supported by us, and for sites with the right volume and deal structure, financed by MPM so there's nothing up front.

What we install

Sized to your tonnage, supported for the life of the program.

How it works

  • Site analysis based on the tonnage your facility actually produces
  • Equipment matched to that tonnage — not the biggest machine we can place
  • At the right volume and deal structure, MPM finances the equipment inside the program agreement
  • Training and ongoing support included; terms discussed site by site, never published

What makes a site a fit

  • Enough monthly volume for the equipment to pay for itself in the material
  • A program term agreement so both sides can plan
  • No loose material on trucks — baled or contained, every load
  • Access to weights and grades so the reporting is real
