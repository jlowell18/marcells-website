Materials

Paper

MPM buys recovered paper from printers, converters, manufacturers and distribution centers — a broad spectrum of grades from newspaper and corrugated to high grades and pulp substitutes. Our direct relationships with domestic and export mills let us work on your behalf and get the most out of every load.

Grades we buy

  • Printers mix, sorted and mixed white ledger, colored ledger and envelope cuttings
  • Coated and uncoated book stock, news blanks and publication blanks
  • Old corrugated containers (OCC), double-lined kraft (DLK) and boxboard
  • Old newspaper, magazines and groundwood grades
  • Hard white, soft white and other pulp substitutes
  • Old inventory of paper — cut sheets and rolls

What happens to your paper

Every load is weighed at our Chicago facility on a computerized scale system, graded, sorted to remove contaminants, and compacted into mill-specification bales. Our facility has immediate, adjacent rail service, so material moves to mills efficiently. Weights and values post to your portal login as loads cross the scale.

Containers and pickup

We supply what your site needs — gaylord boxes, plastic tubs, collapsible containers, bin receptacles or a spotted trailer — and schedule pickups around your production. Same-day service on requests before 10:00 am, six days a week, Sunday on request.

Our sister company, Marcells Pallet Inc, builds, repairs, recycles and sells pallets for the same customers — one pickup, one invoice, paper and pallets together.
