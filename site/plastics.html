Materials

Plastics

Alongside paper and metal, MPM buys industrial scrap plastics from the same plants — so one program and one pickup cover everything leaving your dock.

What we take

  • Stretch film and shrink wrap
  • Plastic strapping
  • Totes, drums, crates and rigid containers
  • Production scrap and off-spec material

How it works

Tell us what the material is and roughly how much you generate. We’ll set up the right container or baling approach, pick it up on your schedule, weigh it on our computerized scale and post it to your portal like any other load.

Not sure if we take it? Call (773) 265-1200 — if it’s plastic and it’s industrial, we’ll tell you straight.
