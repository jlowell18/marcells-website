Services

Everything a recovered-paper program needs, from one company.

MPM works with printers, converters, manufacturers and distribution centers — one plant or forty, local or national. Processing, mill-direct sales, equipment, containers, document destruction and live reporting, all under one program.

Plant Processing

Our Chicago plant grades, weighs and bales recovered paper to mill specification on a computerized scale system, with adjacent rail service to move material efficiently.

Mill Direct

We sell straight to paper mills across the country and overseas, so your price comes from the mill — not from a broker sitting between you and it.

Document Destruction

Sensitive files and materials are collected discreetly and destroyed at an isolated site inside our plant, under 24-hour video surveillance with remote viewing.

Waste Stream Assessment

We walk your floor and look at what's going out — grades, weights, trucks, what's loose — and show you where you're paying to move air and how to stop.

Equipment Financing

For sites with the right volume and deal structure, we finance balers, compactors and conveying equipment within the program.

Online Reporting

Secure, real-time access to your pricing, loads, statements, payments and recycling impact through the customer portal — updated as loads ship.

Nationwide Programs

One program across every site, coast to coast, coordinated from one Chicago operations desk — same grades, same reporting, same people.

Containers & Pickup

Gaylords, plastic tubs, collapsible containers, bins and spotted trailers, with scheduled pickups and same-day service on requests before 10:00 am.

Pallets

Marcells Pallet Inc builds, repairs, recycles and sells wooden pallets for the same customers — one pickup, one invoice.

What a program looks like

01

We look at what you're actually shipping.

Load weights, grades, how many trucks a month, what's going out loose. Most sites are paying to move air.

02

We fix the material before we fix the price.

Baled beats loose by a wide margin — heavier loads, fewer trucks, better grade. If equipment is what's standing in the way, that's our problem to solve.

03

We get the equipment in.

Balers, conveyors, cart tippers, roll cutters — sized to your tonnage. At the right volume and deal structure we finance it within the program.

04

One program across every site.

Same grades, same reporting, same contacts. We coordinate spotted trailers, owner-operators and third-party carriers between sites and mills.

05

You see everything.

Monthly statements per site, and a portal that updates as loads ship — pricing, loads, payments, impact.

Programs built around your plant

Every site is different: different grades, different volumes, different dock. We build the program to fit — the containers, the pickup schedule, the equipment if it's warranted — and we don't publish customer names or use them in our marketing.

Service you can actually reach

You always talk to a person. Same-day service on requests before 10:00 am and never later than the next morning; six days a week, Sunday on request; special pickups dispatched within 24 hours. Prompt payment, and a statement you can audit.
