Frequently asked questions

Does MPM service accounts outside of Illinois?

Yes. MPM runs programs nationwide — every state — and ships to domestic and export mills. See Coverage.

How often do you pick up, and do you operate on weekends?

MPM operates 6 days a week and can arrange Sunday pickups on request. Special pickups can be dispatched within 24 hours by our customer service team, and same-day service is available on requests placed before 10:00 am.

Do you supply the containers for my material?

Yes. MPM provides a full range of containers, from gaylord boxes and collapsible containers to plastic tubs, bin receptacles and spotted trailers dropped at your facility. See Equipment.

What types of materials do you take?

All grades of paper, aluminum plates and mixed metals, industrial scrap plastics, pallets, and secure-destruction documents.

Does MPM buy old paper inventory?

Yes. MPM buys old inventory in both cut sheets and rolls.

How does MPM provide consistent pricing when the market is always changing?

Our direct relationships with both domestic and international mills give us the flexibility to take advantage of movement and price.

Will the price I receive fluctuate?

Recovered paper is a commodity and pricing varies over time with the amount of material on the market. Your current price on every grade is always visible in your portal login.

Can I see my loads and statements?

Every customer gets a login to the MPM portal: pricing by grade, every load with weights and values, monthly statements per site, payment status and recycling impact — updated as loads ship.
