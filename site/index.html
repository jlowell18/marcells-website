What we buy, process and sell

Our primary focus is recovered paper in all grades — OCC, DLK, sorted and mixed ledger, news blanks and publication blanks, book stock, hard white and other pulp substitutes — bought mill-direct and baled to mill specification at our Chicago plant.

While most people know us for paper, MPM also handles aluminum printing plates and mixed metals, industrial scrap plastics, and — through Marcells Pallet Inc — wooden pallets, so one program covers everything leaving your dock.
